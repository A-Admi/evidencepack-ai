RESPONSIBLE USE

The researcher stays in control.

EvidencePack AI organizes evidence and drafts material. It does not scan remote systems, exploit targets, steal credentials, or automatically submit vulnerability reports. Researchers remain responsible for authorization, program scope, factual accuracy, impact claims, and the final submission.
