PRIVACY

Your evidence stays under your control.

Application processing

EvidencePack AI is designed for local processing. The application does not require cloud AI. The Pro edition can optionally communicate with a locally running Ollama service for AI-assisted drafting.

Telemetry

The product is designed without application telemetry or analytics beacons. Evidence should not be uploaded by the application as part of the normal workflow.

Your evidence

Source evidence is treated as researcher-controlled material. You are responsible for making sure you are authorized to possess, process, and share the files you use with the product.

Website and checkout

This public site is designed as a static site without third-party analytics scripts. Hosting providers may process ordinary technical request information according to their own policies. Purchases and downloads are handled through Payhip, whose privacy and payment-processing terms apply when you use its checkout pages.

Sensitive material

Secret detection and redaction suggestions are assistive features and can miss sensitive information. Review every package manually before sharing it.

Contact

For privacy or product questions, use the support/contact method available through the EvidencePack AI Payhip product page.
